Sponsorship Terms

Effective November 2026 · Last updated 2026-11-03

These terms apply to every sponsored video on the Mompreneur Diary (미국주부) YouTube channel. Please read them before reaching out about a collaboration.

1. I test first

I only make videos about tools I can genuinely recommend to my viewers. Before agreeing to a video, I need full access to the paid or upgraded version, usually for 15–30 days (simpler tools may need less). After testing, I will let you know honestly whether I can make the video.

2. What a standard package includes

  • One long-form video — about 5 minutes, in Korean, with subtitles.
  • English dubbing can be added if you would like.
  • One YouTube Short. If testing turns up more good material than fits in the long-form video, I will make an extra Short with it.
  • Other formats and long-term partnerships are welcome — just ask.

I research, test, script, and edit quickly, so the first draft usually comes sooner than you expect.

3. Payment

  • At least 50% of the fee is paid before any work begins. Production starts only after the deposit arrives.
  • Once production has started, the deposit is non-refundable.
  • The deposit applies to every partner, including agencies and large brands. After a past project went unpaid, I no longer start work without one.
  • The balance is due when I send the unlisted draft, within 7 days of delivery.
  • The video goes public only after the full fee has been paid. If the balance is not paid, the video will not be published as a sponsored video, no links or discount codes will be added, and no usage rights are granted.
  • All amounts are in USD. Transfer and payment fees are paid by the sender.

4. Review and changes

  • You will receive an unlisted YouTube link to review before anything goes public.
  • Two rounds of changes are included — tell me what to add or remove.
  • Please send feedback within 5 business days. If I receive no feedback within 5 business days, the draft is considered approved.
  • Requests outside what we agreed (a new concept, a longer video, a different product) are quoted separately.

5. Discount code and affiliate link

Please provide an exclusive discount code for my viewers and, if available, an affiliate link. A code gives viewers a real reason to buy, and it lets both of us see results.

6. Honest opinion

  • You share the key messages, and I write the script.
  • My videos focus on what the tool does well — its benefits and why it helps my viewers — so I usually spend more time on those benefits than on the features alone.
  • Everything I share comes from my own real experience using the tool.
  • If there is a specific feature or number you would like me to include, I would really appreciate it if you could send me the materials.

7. Usage rights

After the full fee is paid, you may share the video and link to it on your own website and social media. Using my video in paid ads (or whitelisting) is a separate agreement.

8. How long the video stays up

Once a video is public, I keep it up. Unless there is a serious problem, I do not take it down.

9. Exclusivity

No exclusivity is included. If you need me to avoid competing products for a period of time, that is quoted separately.

10. Cancellation

  • Before production starts: no charge.
  • After production starts: the deposit is kept.
  • After the draft is delivered: the deposit is kept, and the video and its rights remain with me.

11. Flexible where it matters

Formats, scope, timelines, and pricing are all open to discussion, and I am happy to build a long-term partnership. The deposit, the review window, and payment before publishing are not negotiable.

12. How we agree

Before we start, I will send a short deal summary (what, when, and how much). Replying “Agreed” by email means you accept the summary and these terms.
